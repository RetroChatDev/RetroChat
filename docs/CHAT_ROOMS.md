# Chat Rooms

RetroChat's core is real-time chat. Join public rooms or create your own — including private rooms when you want a closed conversation.

## Finding rooms

The **Rooms** tab lists public rooms. Use the search bar to filter by name or tag. Pin rooms as favorites to keep them at the top of your list.

## Creating a room

Tap **Create Room** and set:

- Name and description.
- Public or private (private rooms require an invite or password).
- Optional password, user limit, tags, and wallpaper.

Room creators automatically become the room's first moderator.

For private conversations, create a **private room** and invite only the people you want. That is the supported private-chat path in RetroChat today.

## Inside a room

- Send text, emoticons, GIFs (via GIPHY), images, files, or a live camera capture.
- Long-press or tap the reply arrow on any message to reply inline.
- Type `@` to mention a user; they get a notification.
- Paste a link and RetroChat auto-generates a preview. Paste a Solana contract address and a token preview appears.
- Tap a user's avatar to open their profile or tip them.

## Room controls

- Favorite the room, mute notifications, change wallpaper, or leave.
- Moderators can pin messages, remove messages, kick, ban, and manage the room's settings.
