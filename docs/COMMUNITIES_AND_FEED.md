# Communities & Feed

Communities are long-lived spaces around a topic, project, or crew. The Feed brings posts from people and communities you follow into one timeline.

## Communities

### Browse and join
The **Communities** tab shows featured, trending, and newly created communities. Tap a community to view its posts, members, and rooms. Tap **Join** to become a member.

### Create a community
Tap **Create Community** and set:
- Name and description.
- Banner and avatar.
- Rules and social links.
- Public or invite-only.

The creator is the first admin and can promote other members to moderator.

### Posts
Inside a community, tap **New Post** to share text, images, or links. Posts support:
- Likes and threaded replies.
- Pinning by moderators.
- Reporting by any member.
- Featured carousel that highlights top posts.

### Roles and moderation
Community admins can assign moderators, manage the member list, and use the moderation panel to review reports and remove content. See [Moderation & Safety](MODERATION_AND_SAFETY.md) for the full toolkit.

### Community Leader workflow
Some communities operate rooms or events under a **Community Leader** role. Members can request the role from the community page, and admins approve or decline. Leaders gain room-management permissions inside the community.

## Feed

The **Feed** tab aggregates posts from communities and users you follow, sorted newest first. Use the filter chips at the top to switch between everything, communities only, or people only. Pull to refresh.
