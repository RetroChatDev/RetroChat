# Learn Center

The Learn Center is RetroChat's built-in help library.

## Browse
Open **Learn** to see articles grouped by category (Getting Started, Wallet, Communities, Live, Token Studio, Safety, and more).

## Search
Use the search bar at the top to search all articles by keyword.

## Read
Each article opens as a mobile-friendly page with headings, images, and links to related articles.

## Contribute
Members can submit new articles or edits from **Learn → Contribute**. Submissions are reviewed before publishing.

## Flag inaccurate content
If an article is out of date or wrong, use **Flag this article** at the bottom of the page. The team is notified and will update or remove it.
