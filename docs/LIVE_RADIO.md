# Live Radio

Live Radio lets you browse and play internet radio stations inside RetroChat while you chat, browse communities, or use other tabs.

## Open Radio

Use the **Radio** control from the dashboard or the floating player. The station picker opens as a full list you can search and filter.

## Browse stations

Switch views in the picker:

- **Popular** — widely listened stations.
- **Trending** — stations gaining attention.
- **Genre** — pick a genre tag (for example rock, electronic, news) to narrow the list.
- **Search** — type a station name, tag, or keyword.
- **Favorites** — stations you saved from the player.
- **Recent** — stations you played recently.

Tap a station to tune in. Playback starts in the RetroChat radio player.

## Player controls

While a station is playing you can:

- Play / pause.
- Skip to the previous or next station in the current list.
- Open the station picker again to change station.
- Favorite or unfavorite the current station.
- See the station name and logo when available.

Audio can keep playing while you navigate to Rooms, Communities, Wallet, or other areas of the app.

## Favorites and recent

Save stations you like from the player so they appear under **Favorites**. **Recent** helps you jump back to something you heard earlier in the session.

## Radio in Spaces

When you host a Community Space, you can pick a radio station as shared soundtrack audio for the Space (where the Space UI offers station pick). Listeners hear the soundtrack according to the Space audio rules.

## Tips

- If a stream fails to load, try another station — some third-party streams go offline.
- Lower device media volume or pause Radio when joining a Live stream or Space so audio does not overlap.
- Radio stations are third-party streams; RetroChat does not operate the stations themselves.
