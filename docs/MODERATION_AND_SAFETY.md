# Moderation & Safety

RetroChat gives every member tools to control their experience and moderators tools to keep spaces healthy.

## Personal safety tools

### Report

Any message, post, or user can be reported. Tap the **⋯** menu and choose **Report**. Pick a reason and add optional context. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team, typically within 24 hours.

### Block

Blocking a user hides their messages, posts, and profile across the app. Manage your block list from your **profile menu → Blocked Users**.

### Mute

Mute a room or community to stop receiving notifications from it without leaving.

## Room moderation

Room creators are the first moderator and can promote others. Moderator tools include:

- Delete any message.
- Pin messages.
- Kick a user from the current session.
- Ban a user from the room.
- Update room settings, password, and wallpaper.

## Community moderation

Community admins and moderators have a dedicated panel:

- Review reported posts and comments.
- Remove content or lock threads.
- Ban members from the community.
- Manage roles (moderator, community leader).

## Age gate

RetroChat requires an age attestation on first sign-in (16+). Creating an in-app wallet requires an 18+ attestation.

## Community guidelines

Every user agrees to the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Repeated or severe violations can result in temporary or permanent account suspension.

## Contact

If you need to escalate a safety issue, email **support@retrochat.io** or use **Report** on the content or profile involved. You can also file a [bug report](FEATURE_REQUESTS_AND_BUGS.md) for product defects (not a substitute for safety reports).
