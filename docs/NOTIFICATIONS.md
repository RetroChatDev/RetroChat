# Notifications

Notifications keep you up to date on mentions, replies, tips, and community events.

## In-app inbox

Tap the bell icon to open the notification inbox. Notifications are grouped by type:

- Mentions and replies in rooms and communities.
- Likes and pins on your posts.
- Tips received.
- Community events (new post from a community you follow, going live, etc.).
- System messages from RetroChat.

## Push notifications

On the native iOS app (and supported Android/PWA setups), RetroChat can send push notifications.

- On first launch you'll be asked to allow notifications.
- Manage per-category preferences under **Settings → Notifications**.
- Turn off all pushes with the master toggle.

## PWA notifications

The installed PWA supports notifications on most desktop browsers and on Android. iOS PWA notification support depends on your iOS version; the native App Store build is the most reliable path for pushes on iPhone.
