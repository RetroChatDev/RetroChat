# Retro TV

Retro TV is RetroChat's built-in video and IPTV experience.

## Open Retro TV

From the dashboard or main navigation, open **Retro TV**. The channel guide is the home view.

## Channel guide

Channels are grouped by category (for example news, music, gaming). Tap a channel to start watching. Use the category tabs or groups across the top to jump between bundles of related channels.

## Video library

Below the guide, the video library shows on-demand clips and recordings. Search by title or filter by tag. Tap an item to play it in the same player experience.

## Now Playing footer

When you start a video and navigate elsewhere in RetroChat, the **Now Playing** mini player docks at the bottom of the screen.

- Tap it to expand back to the full player.
- Swipe down or use the dismiss control to stop and hide it.

This lets you keep watching while you check chat or communities.

## Mobile viewing

On phones you can:

- Swipe left/right on the player to jump between channels.
- Rotate to landscape for full-screen video.

## Channel groups

Curators can bundle related channels into groups (for example "Crypto news" or "Retro gaming") that appear as tabs across the top of the guide.

## Tips

- If a channel fails to load, try another — some streams depend on third-party sources.
- Pause Retro TV before joining a Community Live stream so audio and video do not compete.
- For live community broadcasts (camera or OBS), see [Live Streaming & Spaces](LIVE_STREAMING_AND_SPACES.md) — that is separate from Retro TV channels.
