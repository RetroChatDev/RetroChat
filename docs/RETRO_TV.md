# Retro TV

Retro TV is RetroChat's built-in video and IPTV experience.

## Channel guide

Open **Retro TV** to see the channel guide. Channels are grouped by category (for example news, music, gaming). Tap a channel to start watching.

## Video library

Below the guide, the video library shows on-demand clips and recordings. Search by title or filter by tag.

## Now Playing footer

When you start a video and navigate elsewhere in RetroChat, the **Now Playing** mini player docks at the bottom of the screen. Tap it to expand, swipe down to dismiss.

## Mobile viewing

On phones you can:
- Swipe left/right on the player to jump between channels.
- Rotate to landscape for full-screen video.
- Cast from the player controls (if your device supports it).

## Channel groups

Curators can bundle related channels into groups (for example "Crypto news" or "Retro gaming") that appear as tabs across the top of the guide.
